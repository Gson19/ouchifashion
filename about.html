About

Model, stylist, and designer.

I work across the full image — in front of the camera, behind the looks, and inside the sketchbook. As a model, stylist, and designer, I understand how clothes need to move, feel, and photograph.

On set, I help bring concepts to life with clean, modern silhouettes, thoughtful layering, and attention to detail. In design, I focus on shapes that feel effortless to wear but strong on camera.

Whether we're building a campaign, lookbook, or personal project, my goal is to make every frame feel intentional and true to your brand.
